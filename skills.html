Skills

I design from the feeling outward: what should the player feel, and which systems, sounds, and spaces make that feeling land? In my projects these layers are built to reinforce each other, not to live in separate lanes.

01 Design

  • Systems & Economy Design One shared economy across both loops in Home ?weet Home, where spending is self-soothing rather than progression
  • Core Loop Design A Dual Core Loop in Home ?weet Home that flips a cozy life sim into physics-based action and back
  • Asymmetric Co-op Design Color-filter glasses in SeaTeam split the information so that communication becomes the actual gameplay
  • Game Feel Furniture weight classes in Home ?weet Home: light objects fast and risky, heavy ones slow but hitting as area weapons

02 Process

  • Rapid Prototyping Blizz: from idea to a complete, playable loop solo in one week, without scope creep
  • Playtesting & Iteration Six paper tests in Home ?weet Home and two structured playtests in SeaTeam that directly reshaped the rules
  • Rules & Design Documentation SeaTeam's rulebook rewritten down to about 2.5 pages; the Home ?weet Home GDD documents open gaps honestly
  • Design Lead & Moderation Lead designer on Home ?weet Home and team organization on SeaTeam, moderating a shared direction

03 Sound

  • Sound Design Around 95% of Blizz's audio self-recorded or self-produced, from the dissonant enemy to the warm rain drum
  • Sound as Mechanic In Blizz, hearing is the only navigation channel: a dissonance filter grows as the enemy closes in
  • Audio-Driven Feel A late audio pass in Home ?weet Home that makes collecting currency and landing hits feel tangible

04 Visuals & Space

  • Environmental Storytelling Four decay stages in Home ?weet Home that mirror the protagonist's crumbling psychological facade
  • Visual Readability SeaTeam's color separation hides each player's obstacles while staying legible in print and under time pressure
  • Physical Prototyping Producing the glasses materials, tiles, and boxed final version of SeaTeam as an analog tabletop game
